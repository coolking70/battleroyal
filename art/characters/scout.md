# Scout design sheet

- Age range: approximately 28–32 years old; mature young adult civilian survivor, not a teenager or schoolboy.
- Face and hair: alert narrow face, short dark ash-brown hair, observant expression.
- Clothing: lightweight civilian outdoor jacket, simple layered shirt, plain utility trousers, and a small messenger or observation pouch.
- Palette: slate blue, smoke gray, muted teal.
- Signature prop: compact binoculars or observation device.
- Base portrait policy: unarmed civilian observer; binoculars are the only prominent equipment, with both hands, shoulders, and upper back visibly clear.
- Avoid: oversized equipment, heavy armor, logos, text.
