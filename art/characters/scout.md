# Scout design sheet

- Age range: approximately 28–32 years old; mature young adult civilian observer, not a teenager or schoolboy.
- Face and hair: alert narrow face, short dark ash-brown hair, observant expression.
- Clothing: plain slate-blue outdoor jacket, simple charcoal shirt, plain khaki outdoor trousers, and one small civilian messenger pouch worn at the side.
- Palette: slate blue, smoke gray, muted teal.
- Signature prop: compact binoculars only.
- Base portrait policy: an unarmed civilian urban observer; binoculars are the only visible professional equipment, with both hands, shoulders, and upper back visibly clear.
- Avoid: oversized equipment, heavy armor, logos, text.
