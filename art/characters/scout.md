# Scout design sheet

- Age range: late 20s to early 30s.
- Face and hair: alert narrow face, short dark ash-brown hair, observant expression.
- Clothing: lightweight practical field jacket, layered shirt, compact observation device, utility trousers; no full tactical military loadout.
- Palette: slate blue, smoke gray, muted teal.
- Signature prop: compact binoculars or observation device.
- Avoid: sniper stereotype, camouflage uniform, oversized rifle, heavy armor, logos, text.
