# Fighter design sheet

- Age range: late 20s to mid 30s.
- Face and hair: sturdy face, short practical hair, focused direct expression.
- Clothing: athletic layered streetwear with light protective pads and worn gloves; built for close combat, not a knight or armored soldier.
- Palette: charcoal, rust red, muted warm orange accent.
- Signature prop: wrapped forearm or compact improvised melee guard.
- Avoid: giant muscles, heavy armor, medieval weapons, military insignia, logos, text.
