# Fighter design sheet

- Age range: late 20s to mid 30s.
- Face and hair: sturdy face, short practical hair, focused direct expression.
- Clothing: athletic layered streetwear with light protective pads and worn gloves; built for physical resilience and movement.
- Palette: charcoal, rust red, muted warm orange accent.
- Signature prop: neatly wrapped forearms and simple sport gloves.
- Approved portrait visual identity: adult amateur boxing athlete with a sturdy athletic build, short practical hair, charcoal athletic jacket with muted rust-orange trim, simple boxing wraps and worn training gloves; the approved image reads as a calm civilian sport-trained portrait.
- Avoid: giant muscles, heavy armor, medieval weapons, logos, text.
