# Medic design sheet

- Age range: mid 20s to late 30s.
- Face and hair: calm but tired face, practical tied hair or short hair, attentive expression.
- Clothing: worn everyday survival jacket with a compact medical pouch and restrained green-white accents; practical field medic, not a formal white coat.
- Palette: desaturated green, off-white, gray, muted red detail.
- Signature prop: compact first-aid pouch or folded bandage.
- Approved portrait visual identity: community first-aid worker in a plain desaturated green jacket with off-white panels and a gray shirt, carrying a compact white-and-green first-aid pouch; the approved image reads as an attentive, calm and tired civilian care worker.
- Avoid: sexualized nurse uniform, formal hospital coat, excessive blood, logos, text.
